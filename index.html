Before you start — three things

  1. 1

    These are mock-ups of apps that don't exist

    Thirty-one invented products — no real accounts, balances or transactions. Each screen carries a small LARP · not a real app chip so a screenshot still says what it is.

  2. 2

    The controls are hidden on purpose

    There's no settings icon anywhere — it would spoil the illusion. Instead one panel holds edit values, reset, currency, the home-screen icon and the demo watermark toggle. On a desktop it's the small dot in the bottom-left corner of any dashboard; on a phone, tap three times anywhere on the screen. Each dashboard shows you this once when you open it.

  3. 3

    It runs on your phone too

    Start the Wi-Fi server, scan the QR code, and add any dashboard to your Home Screen with an icon and name of your choosing. Nothing is published to the internet and nothing is uploaded.
